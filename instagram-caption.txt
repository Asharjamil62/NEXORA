NEXORA — a premium electronics storefront concept.

Designed and developed as a portfolio project by VOID GENEX.

The concept brings together a modern dark interface, product browsing, category filters and a curated catalogue across audio, wearables, gaming, displays and accessories.

Built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with product information managed through JSON.

This is a demo concept. Product names, prices and specifications are illustrative and are not available for real purchase.
